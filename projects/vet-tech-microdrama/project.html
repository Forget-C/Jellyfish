Short, comedic AI-generated video content for vet techs, paired with a user-generated-content contest — send us your craziest clinic stories, we turn the best into episodes. Originated by Benny Pugh.

Two phases

Phase 0 — Done

Prove a coherent episode can be made on purpose, reliably — not by accident. A real 6-shot test episode is the evidence.

Phase 2 — Not started

The actual pilot: the UGC contest, 3–5 real episodes, tech-facing-only distribution. Gated on a go/no-go call.

The test episode

"The Case of the Missing Simparica"

A vet tech (Mara) confronts a colleague (Theo) over "eyeballed" inventory counts and a missing medication bin. 6 shots, ~48 seconds — script → shot breakdown → two reusable character keyframes → six image-conditioned video clips with real spoken dialogue → stitched into one file. Same character and setting held consistent across every shot.

Why vet techs

The Inventory Technician persona is TikTok/Instagram-native, wants recognition beyond clinical work, and has real word-of-mouth leverage between techs at different practices — even with zero purchasing authority. The UGC contest plays directly into that: low-cost content supply and a distribution mechanic through exactly the right audience.

Not the audience: economic buyers (Practice Owners, Enterprise Network Managers) respond to ROI and trust signals, not entertainment — and enterprise buyers in particular are known to scrutinize company legitimacy, so this content should stay off channels they'd see.
